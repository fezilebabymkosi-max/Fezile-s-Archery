About Brian Archery

Our Mission

Straight to the Point Archery has been providing high-quality archery education since 2005. As a non-profit archery center in Tacoma, WA, our mission is to teach archery skills, safety, and sportsmanship to archers of all ages and abilities. We offer a positive learning environment where students can develop their skills through group classes, private lessons, and specialized programs. Our certified instructors are dedicated to helping every student reach their full potential, whether they are beginners or advanced archers preparing for competition. At Straight to the Point Archery, we are committed to fostering a community where archery enthusiasts can learn, grow, and enjoy the sport safely.

Our History

Founded in 2005 by archery enthusiast Brian Thompson, Straight to the Point Archery began as a small community program aimed at introducing local youth to the sport of archery. Over the years, we have expanded our offerings to include a wide range of classes, workshops, and events for archers of all skill levels. Our growth has been fueled by our passion for archery and our commitment to providing a safe and supportive environment for learning.

What we do

We are an indoor training facility with 28 shooting lanes (14 ten-yard lanes and 14 twenty-yard lanes) and 2 private coaching rooms. We also have access to 26 additional twenty-yard lanes and an outdoor shooting field. We offer several types of lessons to accommodate everyone from first-time archers to professional archers. Once set up with properly fitting equipment, each archer receives basic safety instruction and is introduced to various shooting techniques.
