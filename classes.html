Our Classes

Our classes are designed to help you develop your archery skills in a supportive and safe environment. Our certified instructors are dedicated to helping every student reach their full potential, whether they are beginners or advanced archers preparing for competition.

Beginner Classes

Our beginner classes are designed for those new to archery. Learn the basics of stance, aiming, and shooting in a supportive environment.

Description: A101 is a basic archery course that focuses on beginning archery safety, skills, and drills. Participants learn about archery form, range rules, and etiquette. Form training and fun games are introduced in this class. Equipment will be provided except for the safety kit. Cost: $120/month Schedule: Mondays and Wednesdays, 4–5 pm Prerequisite: Interest in learning about the sport of archery. Minimum age 8 years old or 7 with pre-evaluation.

Intermediate Classes

For those with some experience, our intermediate classes focus on improving technique, accuracy, and consistency.

Description: A102 is an intermediate archery course that focuses on intermediate archery safety and form. It introduces new equipment styles like shooting recurve and compound bows. Fun games and form reinforcement techniques are emphasized in this class. Equipment will be provided except for the safety kit. Cost: $120/month Schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 4–5 pm Prerequisite: A101 or recommendation from a private lesson instructor. Minimum age 7 years old.

Advanced Classes

Our advanced classes are tailored for seasoned archers looking to refine their skills and compete at higher levels.

Description: After taking the A101 and A102 classes, students are eligible to participate in the A103 class. This class focuses on continued education towards tournament styles of shooting used by many archery associations around the world. Participants receive ongoing guidance on shooting form and advice on personal equipment selections. Equipment will be provided except for the safety kit. Cost: $120/month Schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 5–6 pm Prerequisite: A101/A102 or equivalent. Minimum age 7 years old.

Learn Archery with Our Expert Instructors

Our instructors are qualified Level 1 and Level 2 coaches who have met the safety requirements of USA Archery (USAA). We teach in a positive learning environment. Our instructors also have extensive knowledge of the most advanced archery equipment and all forms of archery, from the traditional longbow to the modern compound bow. We have specific instructors for recreational archery, competitive archery, and bow hunting.
